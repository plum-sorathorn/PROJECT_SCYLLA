# Product

## Register

product

## Platform

web

## Users
Retail derivatives swing traders, active options traders, and portfolio managers tracking large institutional option blocks ("whales") and volatility anomalies.

## Product Purpose
A zero-cost, high-performance unusual options volume scanner and analytics console (featuring Put/Call Ratios, Volume Concentration, IV Skew/Smile, Swing Alignment, and an Expected Move calculator) utilizing public yfinance and OpenBB data.

## Positioning
An elite, keyless derivatives scanner that delivers premium hedge-fund level volatility and volume analysis on a clean, low-latency terminal.

## Brand Personality
Option 3 ("The Leviathan's Wake") — Minimalist luxury, institutional-grade, graphite/gold, editorial typography, quiet confidence, restrained density.

## Anti-references
Flashing "gamer-style" RGB/cyberpunk dashboards with scanline slop, typical "SaaS-cream" landing page templates with giant rounded card grids, decorative glassmorphism.

## Design Principles
- **Quiet Authority**: Visual highlights (like unusual flow) should be presented with high-end, restrained cues (e.g. thin gold accents, platinum styling) rather than screaming neon blurs.
- **Actionable Density**: Maximize high-data table visibility while maintaining balanced typography (sans-serif data, serif headers).
- **Functional Transitions**: Animations must solely convey state changes (like data updates or socket pings) and never serve as pure decoration.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG AA compliance (especially minimum 4.5:1 contrast on graphite/gold text combinations), responsive spacing hierarchy, and support for user preferences for reduced motion (e.g. instantly resolving radar/table updates rather than drawing animations).
